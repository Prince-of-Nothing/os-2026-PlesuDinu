# Lab Terminal Evidence

The `.typescript` files are recordings of interactive xv6 console sessions captured with `script` and QEMU. The `.txt` files preserve command output from Bash, xv6, and the Lab 5 exercise runner. These are useful for checking report answers, but they are not screenshots. The UTM logo is extracted from the provided sample report for the cover.

The Lab 3 report now embeds five genuine Konsole screenshots from the `real/` folder: Part 1 command lookup, Part 2 shell expansion, Part 3 redirection and chaining, the Part 4 one-liner run, and the Part 5 script run. They were captured from the live desktop using Spectacle and are not rendered from text. The remaining Lab 3 figure slots stay empty because separate screenshots were not captured. Lab 4/5 figure slots also remain placeholders where real captures are still needed.

For Lab 3, five genuine Konsole screenshots are now in `real/`: Part 1 lookup/PATH/status, Part 2 expansions and quoting, Part 3 redirection/chaining/jobs, Part 4 full one-liner run, and Part 5 report.sh. These were captured from the live desktop terminal with Spectacle and embedded in the corresponding DOCX figures. Other Lab 3 figure slots remain blank because separate real captures were not taken.

`capture-lab3.sh` and `capture-lab4-bash.sh` rerun the local Bash examples and refresh their text captures. `lab4-xv6.typescript` and `lab4-sleep.typescript` are the original xv6 console recordings.
